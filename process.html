Tea Production Process

  1. Harvesting: The first step involves picking the young tea leaves and buds during the right season to ensure quality.
  2. Withering: The freshly picked leaves are left to wither for several hours to reduce moisture content and enhance flavor.
  3. Pan-Firing: The withered leaves are then pan-fired to halt oxidation and preserve the green color.
  4. Shaping: The leaves are rolled or shaped to enhance their appearance and ensure even drying.
  5. Drying: The shaped leaves are dried to remove any remaining moisture and prevent spoilage.
  6. Sorting/Grading: Finally, the dried tea leaves are sorted and graded based on size and quality for packaging and selling.

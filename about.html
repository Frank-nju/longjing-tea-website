About Longjing Tea

History

Longjing tea, also known as Dragon Well tea, is a famous green tea originating from Hangzhou, Zhejiang province, China. It has a history of more than 1200 years and is renowned for its distinctive flat leaves and a rich, grassy flavor. During the Song Dynasty, it was designated as a tribute tea and has since been cherished by Chinese emperors and tea aficionados alike.

Cultural Significance

Longjing tea is deeply embedded in Chinese culture, symbolizing hospitality and respect. Traditionally served during gatherings and festivals, it is often used in ceremonies to honor guests. The art of brewing and serving Longjing tea has been perfected over centuries and is considered a cultural heritage in China. In addition, Longjing tea has been recognized for its health benefits, contributing to its esteemed status in both cultural and societal contexts.
